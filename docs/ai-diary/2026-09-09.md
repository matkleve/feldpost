## Went wrong

- **My own reduced-motion rule from yesterday froze spinners.** The exemption
  was `:not([class*="__spinner"])`, chosen after looking at two spinner
  stylesheets and generalising from them. There are **nine** spinner classes;
  three do not carry the `__spinner` tail — `.upload-panel__thumbnail-spinner`,
  `.invite-editor__qr-spinner`, and the `hlm-spinner` primitive, which has
  `animate-spin` and no BEM class at all. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` those
  three stopped after one rotation, which reads as a hung app — the exact
  failure the exemption existed to prevent.
  **The lesson is about the shape of the check, not the count:** I wrote an
  exemption keyed on a naming convention after sampling two examples, instead of
  enumerating every element the rule would hit. A rule that suppresses behaviour
  globally needs its exception list derived from a full search, not from the
  cases that prompted it. `rg -l 'animation:[^;]*spin'` was one command and
  would have shown all eight SCSS cases immediately.
  Fixed by matching `spinner` anywhere in the class list plus `animate-spin`,
  and verified in the built CSS.

## Decisions

- **The primitives are in better shape than the design audit implied.** Six of
  the seven genuinely interactive primitives carry every applicable state. The
  audit's § S1 framing ("the primary action has no press feedback") was true of
  `button` before yesterday and was never true of the layer as a whole. Recorded
  so the next reader does not go looking for a systemic problem that is not there.
- **Inline `color-mix` in the primitive TS is not going to be fixed by the
  existing mixin.** A `cva` string cannot `@include` Sass. The answer is shared
  exported constants in TS — which `quietInteractionEmphasis` and
  `toggleOffEmphasis` already approximate — plus a guard that admits it is not
  looking at `.ts` rather than staying silent.

## Findings worth acting on

- `guard-interaction-emphasis.mjs` scans only `.scss`. Seven of its own banned
  patterns sit in the primitive `.ts` files, invisible to it.
- `registry.json`'s nine state-declaring entries and the 17 primitives are
  **disjoint sets** — no primitive declares states, so the proposed
  "assert declared states are implemented" gate has nothing to check yet.
- `shared/ui/card/` and `shared/ui/toast/` are imported by nothing. Toast is
  actively misleading: a working toast system lives in `shared/toast/`.
- `badge` and `select` use `focus:` rather than `focus-visible:` — a ring after
  every mouse click.
- Nine primitives have no dedicated spec, including `input` (56 usages) and
  `label` (49).
